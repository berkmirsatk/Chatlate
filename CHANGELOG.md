# Changelog

## 1.3.0

### Translation

- Translate articles (Instant View), long ones included, with a translate bar like the one in chats. Its menu switches services, translating the article again, and hides it, showing the original; Translate in the article menu brings it back.

### Providers

- DeepL is removed; it was too limited to be useful.

## 1.2.0

### Translation

- Translate your draft into a chosen language by long-pressing Send.

### Providers

- Add DeepL translation support. Usage may be temporarily limited.
- Improve handling of large Google translation requests.

### Improvements and fixes

- Fix in-app update installation.
- Various fixes and improvements to translation and settings.

## 1.1.0

### Providers

- Microsoft Translator joins Google and Yandex.
- Switch services from the translate bar's menu in any chat.

### Translation

- When a service does not answer, the next one takes over within seconds, and a short note in the chat says so.
- Group, channel and profile descriptions translate in place instead of in a popup.
- Inugram's Show original text setting now also covers whole-chat translation.
- The translate bar no longer appears for chats already in your language, and Don't translate works without Premium. Any chat can still be translated from its menu.

### Settings

- Release notes can be translated into the app's language with one tap.

## 1.0.0

The first release of Chatlate: chat translation for Inugram that keeps messages looking the way they were written.

### Translation

- Translate single messages or whole chats, no Telegram Premium needed.
- Bold, italics, links, quotes and spoilers stay in place.
- Code, usernames and custom emoji are never changed.
- Group chats that mix languages are translated message by message.

### Providers

- Use Google, Yandex or both, and drag them into your preferred order.
- If a service is unavailable, the next one takes over automatically.
- Test the connection to see each service's status and response time.

### Settings

- English and Turkish interface that follows Inugram's language.
- Your choices are kept across restarts and updates.
- Check for updates and read the version history in settings.
- Turning Chatlate off returns to Telegram's translation; turning it on restores your choice.
- Community links and support options are one tap away on the settings banner.
